# 03a — Market & Pricing Research: Indian Restaurant Software

Prepared: 31 Oct 2026 · Owner: Manhar Creatives · Scope: competitor price points, add-on/hardware costs, complaints, payment-rail facts.

**How to read this file.** Almost no Indian restaurant-software vendor publishes a rate card; they sell through demos. Every number below comes from a *third-party listing or a competitor's blog*, not from the vendor. Each row carries a confidence tag:
- **[V]** = vendor's own page shows the number (Zoho, Cashfree).
- **[T]** = third-party directory/listing (Techjockey, SaaSworthy, SoftwareSuggest) — plausible, unverified.
- **[B]** = a rival vendor's marketing blog (dineopen, dineomai, orderitnow, dinelax, knomi). These authors sell competing products; treat as *directional only*.
- **[?]** = conflicting or unstated — do not quote publicly.

Nothing here should be quoted on the Nexus landing page as a competitor price. Use it internally for positioning only.

### What the table tells us
1. **The price floor is moving to ₹500–₹1,300 per outlet per month** (Zoho paid plans, low-cost entrants). The "₹10,000 a year" anchor (≈₹830/mo) set by Petpooja/GoFrugal is the number every Indian owner has heard.
2. **Basic billing is nearly free** (Zoho's free tier; Dinelax ₹149). Nexus cannot win on "cheap billing". It must win on *what happens between the table and the report* — Service Requests, KOS, Sold-Out, controlled Void/Refund, Activity History.
3. **Nobody publishes a transparent rate card** (Petpooja, Restroworks, Torqus, LimeTray, GoFrugal-per-module). A published, all-in INR price with GST stated is itself a differentiator.
4. Mid-market realistic spend per outlet is **₹10,000–₹30,000/yr** for software (Petpooja/GoFrugal with a few add-ons) and **₹6,000–₹25,000/yr** on Zoho paid plans.

## 4. Customer complaints — what owners actually say

Evidence is thin and skewed (most review pages show 2020–2025 reviews; none is a systematic survey). Patterns that recur:

| Theme | Evidence | Source |
|---|---|---|
| **Support responsiveness** | Capterra: 1-star review (7 May 2024) says vendor slow after taking advance payment; other reviewers (Aug 2024, Aug 2025) ask for faster response. Techjockey: phone lines hard to reach, long hold; SMS sender-ID approvals slow (2020). Restroworks: smaller restaurants report slower support (enterprise focus). | capterra.com/p/172163 (page updated 29 Oct 2026); techjockey.com/reviews/petpooja-pos; orderitnow.in (24 Jun 2026) |
| **Opaque pricing / add-on stacking** | "Does not publish transparent pricing"; owners "pay for features they never use"; higher tier needed for captain app, KDS, QR; billing term unstated. | orderitnow.in; dineopen.com (21 Oct 2026) |
| **Slow setup** | Restroworks: "takes weeks, not days" — menu setup, training, integrations need dedicated sessions. | orderitnow.in (24 Jun 2026) |
| **Peak-hour slowness** | Capterra reviewer (Aug 2025): system slows at peak, hurts billing speed. | capterra.com/p/172163 |
| **Online-order / availability toggles fail** | Item/add-on on/off toggles sometimes fail (Dec 2022); online orders appear only on the server (Aug 2024). | capterra.com/p/172163 |
| **Order-entry friction & menu upkeep** | Hard order screen, many clicks for KOT/bill (2021); menu updates manual & time-consuming; inventory hard to learn. | capterra.com/p/172163 |
| **Integration / open API limits** | "No open APIs seen" (2021 reviews). | capterra.com; techjockey |
| **Lock-in & exit fees** | Generic claim of ₹5,000–₹15,000 exit fees and hardware lock-in (Torqus); unsourced. | orderitnow.in |
| **Renewal creep** | Advice: write renewal rate into contract — first-year discounts common. | knomi.in |
| **Printer issues / offline / Tally-GST** | **Not found** in any review source reviewed (printer mapping was praised). The brief's suspicion is plausible but I could not verify complaints; do not claim competitors fail here. | — |

**Marketing implications (honest ones):** publish all-in pricing and say GST is extra; promise a data export on exit at no charge; avoid claiming offline billing (ADR-009 — Nexus is interruption-tolerant, not offline); don't claim better support than competitors until we have pilots to prove it.
